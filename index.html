    How it works

    1. Add a task — type what needs doing, and optionally who it's for and when it's due.
    2. Stay on top of it — overdue tasks are flagged, and the counters show your open, done and overdue totals at a glance.
    3. Tick it off — check the box when a task is complete. Use the search and filters to find anything fast.
    4. It's saved for you — every change is stored securely in the cloud, so your list is waiting when you come back on any device.
